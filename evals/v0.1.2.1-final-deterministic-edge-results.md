# v0.1.2.1 Final Deterministic Edge Results

## Changed

The canonical v0.1.2.1 contract closes the final deterministic edges: deferred reasons must be meaningful, supersession graphs cannot self-reference or cycle, historical coverage cannot create current closure gaps, and screenless behavior is explicitly requirement-level.

- Unknown and decision deferrals require a non-empty, non-placeholder `deferral_reason` in addition to source and the eight-axis review.
- `superseded_by` retains existing same-type target validation and now rejects self-reference and graph cycles.
- Closure coverage metrics inspect active material requirement rows only; historical rows remain structurally validated.
