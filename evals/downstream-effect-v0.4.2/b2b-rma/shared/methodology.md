# Methodology

## Experimental question

Given the same approved Product Definition, native Figma authority, human-readable handoff, stack, and correction budget, determine whether giving Treatment a reviewed executable downstream conformance contract before implementation materially improves implementation conformance.

## Isolation and ordering

1. The controller verified the exact frozen baseline and created a clean detached worktree.
2. A fresh Product Agent received only the Korean one-sentence product request.
3. Every Product Agent question passed a private `REQUESTED_ONLY_CHECK`; only explicitly requested clauses were disclosed.
4. The mandatory final-resolution reversal was injected only after the initial irreversible decision had materially propagated.
5. Product Definition was closed and independently revalidated before this evidence directory was created.
6. Native Figma, S0, the Treatment contract, S1, both implementations, and both audits remain ordered gates. Neither implementation may begin before S1 exists.

## Fixed controls

- Baseline and generic downstream subsystem remain frozen.
- One canonical Product Definition and one audited native editable Figma design are shared by both arms.
- Control never receives the executable Treatment contract before or during implementation.
- Treatment receives the independently reviewed full contract before implementation.
- C0 and T0 must both be frozen before cross-arm comparison or feedback.
- Each arm receives at most three targeted correction passes after both blind initial audits are frozen.

## Evidence standard

Success requires exact artifacts, hashes, readback, official validators, semantic traceability, runtime behavior, executable sequences, and visual/responsive verification where applicable. Filenames, narration, green builds, isolated helper tests, and screenshots alone are not conformance proof.
